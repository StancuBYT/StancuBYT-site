1. Executive Summary

StancuBYT (SBYT) is an ERC‑20 token created to support a modern, transparent, and utility-oriented digital community. With a total supply of 4,000,000 SBYT and a launch price of 200,000 SBYT = 1 ETH, SBYT is designed to provide real utility — internal payments, rewards, access to services, and community participation. The official launch took place on October 26, 2025.
